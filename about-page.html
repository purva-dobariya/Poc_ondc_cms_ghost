An open network for digital commerce

The Open Network for Digital Commerce is an initiative designed to democratize digital commerce and create opportunities for businesses of different sizes.

Instead of requiring buyers and sellers to use the same application, an open network allows different participants to connect and interact.

This creates a more interoperable and competitive digital commerce ecosystem.

What ONDC enables

  • Open participation
  • Interoperability
  • Greater discoverability
  • More choices for consumers
  • Opportunities for businesses
